The details

  • Base object: Hand-selected reclaimed glass jug, cleaned & rewired
  • Shade: 3D-printed PLA lattice diffuser, warm-white finish
  • Foot: Solid oak, hand-oiled
  • Bulb: Dimmable E27 LED included
  • Cable: 2m fabric-braided cord, EU/UK plug on request
  • Certification: CE rewired & safety-tested
  • Edition: Numbered, one-of-a-kind piece

Care & story

Every Loom1 carries the dents, patina and quirks of its former life as a humble glass jug. Wipe the shade gently with a dry cloth — the printed lattice is sturdy but enjoys a soft touch. Because each base object is reclaimed individually, no two Loom1 lamps are perfectly alike.
